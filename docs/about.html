About Soulbook

A fan-made field guide to Deadlock.

◆ What this is

Soulbook collects what you need between matches: hero and item tier lists built from real match results, every hero's abilities and lore, the full item shop, and a workshop for planning builds stage by stage.

◆ Where the data comes from

Hero, ability and item data, and all match statistics, come live from deadlock-api.com, a free community project. The site refreshes its copy every few hours, so stats and item numbers follow Valve's patches without anyone editing this site.

The tier list ranks heroes mostly by win rate over the chosen period, with a small bonus for how often they are picked. It is a snapshot of what is winning right now, not a judgment of skill ceilings.

◆ Using builds in game

Every hero page shows the community builds most players actually picked in matches over the last two weeks, with the items most of them buy marked as recommended. Open one in the workshop to use it as a starting point.

Deadlock has no import code for builds from outside the game. Plan your build here, press Copy list, and recreate it in the in-game build editor in the same order. Share links keep the whole build in the URL, so nothing is stored on a server. Each hero keeps its own draft while the site is open; saved builds stay in your browser.

◆ Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Game data is cached in your browser for a few hours so pages load faster, and your drafts and saved builds live only in your browser. The only outside services your browser contacts are deadlock-api.com for game data and Google Fonts for the typefaces; neither learns anything about what you do on this site.
